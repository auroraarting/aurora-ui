Graduate Analyst Programme

EMEA Graduate Analyst

Join Aurora's analytical community across Europe, the Middle East and Africa — producing forecasts and strategic advice that shape the global energy transition.

EMEA locations — Graduate Analyst

Locations

About the programme

The Graduate Analyst Programme gives you an immersive introduction to energy markets analysis. You'll work alongside senior analysts on live client projects — producing forecasts, reports and strategic advisory that directly shape investment decisions across the global energy sector.

What you'll do

  • Analyse power markets, grid infrastructure and policy across your region
  • Build and maintain quantitative models for energy price forecasting
  • Contribute to client reports and presentations
  • Collaborate cross-functionally with modelling and software teams

What we look for

  • Strong quantitative and analytical ability
  • Degree in economics, engineering, mathematics, physics or related field
  • Intellectual curiosity about energy markets and the energy transition
  • Excellent written and verbal communication

Graduate Modeller Programme

EMEA Graduate Modeller

Build the quantitative engine behind Aurora's forecasts — sitting at the intersection of energy systems, mathematics and cutting-edge computational methods.

EMEA locations — Graduate Modeller
General overview of opportunities

Programme length

2 years

Start date

September 2026

Region

EMEA

Locations

Oxford · Berlin

About the programme

Graduate Modellers develop Aurora's proprietary large-scale simulations of electricity markets and grid infrastructure. You'll work with world-class researchers and engineers to produce the forecasts that underpin strategic decisions for clients worldwide.

What you'll do

  • Develop and validate energy system models in Python and other tools
  • Improve forecast accuracy through statistical analysis and calibration
  • Work with large, complex datasets from grid operators and market exchanges
  • Collaborate with analysts and software engineers in cross-functional teams

What we look for

  • Strong mathematical, statistical or computational background
  • Degree in mathematics, physics, engineering, computer science or related
  • Programming experience (Python preferred)
  • Interest in energy systems and market design

EMEA Graduate Software Engineer

About the programme

Graduate Software Engineers at Aurora build and maintain the EOS platform, internal tools and the data pipelines that support our models and analytics. You'll work within agile product teams, contributing to real features that thousands of energy professionals use every day.

What you'll do

  • Develop full-stack features for Aurora's EOS analytics platform
  • Design and maintain scalable data pipelines and APIs
  • Collaborate with modellers and analysts to surface insights effectively
  • Participate in code reviews, testing and architectural decisions

What we look for

  • Degree in computer science, software engineering or related field
  • Proficiency in Python and/or JavaScript/TypeScript
  • Understanding of software design principles and data structures
  • Collaborative, product-minded approach to engineering
